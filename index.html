About

Ben Sirb is an AI researcher at Interval and a mathematician by training. A lifelong love of math took him through a PhD in decentralized machine learning, which he defended in 2018.

His interest in decentralized networking and machine learning has shaped every step since his defense. At Storj Labs he modeled object durability for a decentralized storage network. At Chainlink Labs he modeled BFT consensus mechanisms. At Interval he now works on applied AI, the side of the field he finds most compelling.

Across those roles the constant is a first-principles approach and a focus on how technologies fit together for their users rather than on tuning any single model. That keeps him at the intersection of product, research and engineering, building AI that is accessible, ethical and privacy-preserving.
