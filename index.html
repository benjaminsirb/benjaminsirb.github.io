About

Hi! I'm Ben, and no LLM was used in the generation of this intro. I'm a (former) mathematician and an all-around computer nerd. How did I get here? Well, it all began a long time ago... I've liked math since I was young (and I was relatively good at it), so I always found others' aversion to it unfortunate. I initially decided to study math because I wanted to help people see math as a friendly pursuit, not a daunting one. My first goal was to teach at the high school level, but I found my coursework too fascinating to stop at my B.S., so I decided to pursue my Master's, and then my PhD, which I defended in 2018.

My PhD led me to research in the area of decentralized machine learning, which was a perfect combination of two fields I found interesting: AI and decentralized networking. After my defense, I first worked at Storj Labs helping model object durability on the Storj network, and then at Chainlink Labs where I helped model BFT consensus mechanisms. While I'm still interested in decentralized networking, I have become a lot more interested in the applications of AI, which is what I am working on currently at Interval.

My problem-solving approach is based on starting from first principles. While many people with my background enjoy working in the weeds (e.g. tuning hyperparameters of a ML model and optimizing for measures of accuracy), I'm more interested in making technologies work together for its users. This puts me at the nexus of product, research, and engineering, in any role I take on. I'm thankful that I get to work on accessible, ethical, privacy-preserving AI, and I hope to continue my pursuits for the foreseeable future. Thanks for reading!
